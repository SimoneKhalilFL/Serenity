# StayAtFlorida Brand Standards

**Version 1.0** · Adopted July 2026

This document is the single source of truth for how StayAtFlorida sounds, looks, and behaves — on the website, in listings, on OTA platforms, in guest communication, in décor decisions, and in every future property we bring into the collection.

Every line of copy, photo, and pricing decision on the site should be measured against these standards. If it doesn't fit here, it doesn't ship.

---

## Our Mission

To create unforgettable beachfront vacations where families reconnect, relax, and build lifelong memories.

We don't simply rent vacation homes.
We create places people can't wait to return to.

## Vision

To become the most trusted owner-hosted luxury beachfront vacation brand on Florida's Gulf Coast.

Guests should immediately recognize a StayAtFlorida Signature Property for its quality, thoughtful design, exceptional communication, and memorable experience.

## Brand Promise

> **Where unforgettable beach memories begin.**

Every decision should support this promise.

## Brand Personality

If StayAtFlorida were a person...

**It would be:**

- ✓ Warm
- ✓ Relaxed
- ✓ Sophisticated
- ✓ Trustworthy
- ✓ Welcoming
- ✓ Professional

**Never:**

- ✗ Flashy
- ✗ Loud
- ✗ Salesy
- ✗ Overly casual
- ✗ Cheesy

---

## Tone of Voice

Always write like a boutique hotel.
Never like a real estate listing.

**Instead of:**
> Fully equipped kitchen.

**Write:**
> Gather around after a day on the beach to prepare breakfast, share family dinners, or enjoy a quiet evening overlooking the Gulf.

**Instead of:**
> King bedroom.

**Write:**
> Drift off after watching the sunset from your private balcony and wake up refreshed for another day at the beach.

Never exaggerate. Never overpromise. Always be authentic.

---

## Writing Style

**Use:**

- Luxury
- Comfort
- Experiences
- Views
- Memories
- Hospitality
- Connection
- Relaxation

**Avoid:**

- Paradise
- Dream Vacation
- Amazing
- Best Ever
- Perfect
- Luxury!!! (with exclamation points)

Those words are overused and less credible.

---

## Photography Standards

Every photo must answer:

> "What will my vacation feel like?"

**Priority order:**

1. View
2. Lifestyle
3. Bedrooms
4. Living room
5. Kitchen
6. Dining
7. Balcony
8. Beach
9. Amenities
10. Bathrooms

**Photography style:**

- Natural light
- Warm tones
- Minimal clutter
- Authentic
- Inviting
- No HDR
- No unrealistic editing
- No fake blue water

### Lifestyle Photos

Every StayAtFlorida property should include:

- ☕ Morning coffee
- 🥂 Sunset drinks
- 🍽 Dinner with a view
- 🏖 Beach setup
- 🎲 Family game night
- 📚 Quiet reading spot
- 🌅 Sunrise or sunset

These sell emotions.

## Internal Decision Filter

Before making any change — website, listing, décor, pricing, messaging — ask these five questions:

1. Does this strengthen the StayAtFlorida brand?
2. Does it help a guest picture themselves on vacation?
3. Does it justify our premium pricing?
4. Is it authentic and sustainable?
5. Would a guest remember this six months after their stay?

If the answer is "no" to most of those questions, don't do it.
