## 12. Approved CTAs

Pick one primary and one secondary per surface. Never use OTA-forbidden channel steering on Airbnb/VRBO/Booking.

- **Direct site primary:** `Book Direct & Save`
- **Direct site secondary:** `View Photos`, `Email to Book`
- **Airbnb / VRBO / Booking:** `Message the host`, `See more photos`, `Check availability` (platform-native only)

Never use: `Book Now!`, `Reserve Now`, `Get 20% Off`, `Claim your stay`.

## 13. Master Short Description

*30–45 words. One experiential sentence, ending in a period, no CTA. This is the source for the property card blurb on the website and the "short summary" fields on every OTA (trimmed to their character limits).*

*Example (Twenty First): "A three-bedroom beachfront retreat above the emerald Gulf, designed for families who want to slow down, watch the water, and reconnect."*

## 14. Master Long Description

*4–6 paragraphs, ~250–400 words. Boutique-hotel voice. Structure:*

**Paragraph 1 — the feel.** *One experiential sentence, one image, and the property's name in the second sentence.*

**Paragraph 2 — the home.** *Layout, sleeping arrangements, the feel of the space.*

**Paragraph 3 — the view and the beach.** *Balcony, view, direct beach access, and the complimentary chairs/umbrella sentence verbatim.*

**Paragraph 4 — the resort / community.** *Pools, fitness, grounds, location context.*

**Paragraph 5 — practicalities.** *Parking, check-in, Wi-Fi, cleaning fee scope.*

**Paragraph 6 — the host.** *Owner-hosted. Reply within 24 hours. Direct booking option.*

## 16. Approved House Rules Language

- No smoking, no vaping, no pets *(unless the property is pet-friendly)*
- No parties, events, or unregistered guests
- Primary booker must be 25 or older
- Max occupancy is the platform-listed sleeping capacity
- Quiet hours 10:00 PM – 8:00 AM per HOA
- Damages: honest disclosure resolves nearly everything; undisclosed damage is charged

## 17. Approved Cancellation Language

*One sentence, factual. Match the platform's policy tier where OTAs mandate one. Direct-site policy stated plainly.*

- **Direct site:** *e.g., "Full refund up to 30 days before check-in. 50% refund 15–30 days out. No refund within 14 days."*
- **Airbnb:** *e.g., "Firm cancellation policy — see Airbnb listing for exact terms."*
- **VRBO:** *e.g., "Moderate cancellation policy — see VRBO listing for exact terms."*
- **Booking.com:** *e.g., "Non-refundable within 30 days of check-in."*

Never claim "free cancellation any time" if it isn't true.

## 19. Review Response Style

- Public replies are two to three sentences max.
- Thank the guest by first name.
- Reference one specific detail from their review.
- No boilerplate ("Thank you for your wonderful review!!").
- For constructive reviews: acknowledge the issue, state the fix, don't argue.

See [`../../brand/HOSPITALITY.md#11-public-review-response-on-platform`](../../brand/HOSPITALITY.md#11-public-review-response-on-platform) for full templates.
