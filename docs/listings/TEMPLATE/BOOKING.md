## Booking.com-specific rules

- **Tone:** factual, hotel-style. Less "boutique-hotel warmth," more "concise-informative." Booking's users are comparison-shopping across dozens of similar properties.
- **Never** mention `stayatflorida.com`, `book direct`, or fee-comparison messaging.
- **Never** promise "free cancellation" unless the actual policy allows it.
- **Never** invent a star rating — Booking assigns its own display rating from guest reviews.
- Keep amenity claims strictly aligned with the amenity checkbox Booking uses; discrepancies here trigger review flags.
