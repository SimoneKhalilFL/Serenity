## VRBO-specific rules

- Never mention `stayatflorida.com`, `book direct`, or `save fees` in the listing body — VRBO forbids channel steering.
- VRBO indexes long descriptions for search; use natural keyword integration (location, "beachfront," "family") without stuffing.
- Never claim a specific award, ranking, or "Premier Host" badge unless we hold it.
