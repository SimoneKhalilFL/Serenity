## Long Description (~395 words)

### Overview

Twenty First is a luxury 3-bedroom, 3-bath beachfront condo in Panama City Beach, Florida, inside Tidewater Beach Resort. The condo sleeps up to 8 guests and features panoramic Gulf views from a private beachfront balcony, direct beach access from the resort deck, and full resort amenities.

### Accommodation

The condo offers three separate sleeping areas: a primary bedroom with a king bed and en-suite bathroom, a guest bedroom with a queen bed and adjacent bathroom, and a bunk room with one set of twin bunks and its own bathroom. A queen sleeper sofa in the living room accommodates one to two additional guests. In-unit amenities include a full kitchen, dishwasher, washer and dryer, smart TVs with streaming in the living room and every bedroom, and Wi-Fi throughout.

### Location

Panama City Beach on Florida's Emerald Coast. Direct beach access from the resort deck. Publix grocery under 1 mile. Panama City Beach's Pier Park is a short drive, as is St. Andrews State Park (departure point for the Shell Island shuttle). Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport (ECP) is approximately 35 minutes by car.

## Booking.com-specific rules

- Tone is factual and hotel-style. Less "boutique-hotel warmth," more "concise-informative."
- Never mention `stayatflorida.com`, `book direct`, or fee-comparison messaging.
- Never promise "free cancellation" unless the actual rate policy allows it.
- Never invent a star rating — Booking assigns its own display rating from guest reviews.
- Keep amenity claims strictly aligned with Booking's amenity checkbox — discrepancies trigger review flags.
