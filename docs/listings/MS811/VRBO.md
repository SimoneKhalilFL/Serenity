## Headline — locked ship string *(Phase 3 initiative #1, 2026-07-09)*

**`Westlight · Gulf-Front 2BR · Sleeps 6 · Miramar Beach`** *(53 / 80 chars)*

**Copy this exact string into the VRBO listing editor's `Headline` field.** Middots are `·` (U+00B7), not periods. No trailing punctuation.

**Ship rationale:**

- **Same brand prefix as the Airbnb title.** The first 26 characters (`Westlight · Gulf-Front 2BR`) match the Airbnb title exactly — deliberate. A guest who cross-shops the same brand across Airbnb and VRBO (which many do, especially for high-consideration bookings) reads the same brand+differentiator on both platforms and recognizes it as the same listing. Reduces the "are these the same place?" confusion that kills conversions.
- **VRBO extension adds sleeps + city.** VRBO surfaces the city less prominently than Airbnb on search tiles, so we include `Miramar Beach` in the headline itself. `Sleeps 6` speaks to VRBO's core audience (families and groups booking a whole home, filtering by guest count).
- **Middot separators throughout.** Consistent visual rhythm; same glyph as the Airbnb title.
- **Under the 80-char hard limit.** The full string runs 53 chars — well inside the hard cap and inside the ~60-char practical mobile-tile window. The `Westlight · Gulf-Front 2BR` opening runs 26 chars — under the soft 40-char threshold that keeps the brand + core differentiator visible on truncated tile views.
- **No fluff, no adjectives.** `Luxury`, `Beautiful`, `Stunning`, `Family Retreat` — all deliberately absent. VRBO's search-signal weight is on specifics (bedroom count, sleeps, city, feature) not adjectives (per the [`Poor examples`](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-I-list-my-property) VRBO itself calls out in its listing guidance).
- **Portfolio consistency.** Twenty First's VRBO headline uses the same formula: `Twenty First · Gulf-Front 3BR · Sleeps 8 · Panama City Beach`. Same shape, different city + bedroom + sleeps values.

**Owner sign-off:** 2026-07-09 evening.

**Never use:** floor number (8th, 8th-floor, floor 8), unit number (811, MS811, condo 811), retired brand names (`Majestic Sun 811` as guest-facing brand, `Fun in the Sun`, `Serenity`), resort name as a lead (`Majestic Sun Resort · Westlight …`), all-caps, exclamation marks, or promotional language (`Book Now`, `Best rate`, `Special offer`).

## Long Description

### Welcome

Welcome to Westlight, where every evening ends in gold. This 2-bedroom, 2-bath Gulf-front retreat sits directly overlooking the Gulf of Mexico, where the emerald water meets sugar-white sand and the last light of the day pours across the balcony. A slower, quieter stretch of Florida's Emerald Coast — designed to be lived in barefoot, coffee in hand, sunset ahead.

Westlight was created for guests who want the comforts of home paired with a true beachfront experience on the Emerald Coast. A full kitchen, real bedrooms, in-unit laundry, and a private Gulf-front balcony — the everyday rhythm of home, only with the Gulf a boardwalk away.

### The Home

Westlight is a 2-bedroom, 2-bath condominium that comfortably sleeps up to 6:

- **Primary bedroom:** King bed with Gulf-facing windows
- **Guest bedroom:** Queen bed, tucked away for privacy
- **Living room sleeper sofa:** Queen sleeper; fits 1–2 additional guests
- **Sleeps up to 6 guests total**

Inside, the kitchen is fully stocked for six — coffee maker, cookware, and dinnerware for real cooking, not just reheating. Dining table seats six. Smart TVs with popular streaming apps available in every bedroom and the living room. Wi-Fi reaches every room. In-unit washer and dryer.

### The View & Beach

The private Gulf-front balcony is the heart of Westlight. Morning coffee overlooking the Gulf. Afternoons listening to the waves. Evenings watching unforgettable sunsets that inspired the name Westlight. Beach access is a short walk downstairs — cross Scenic Gulf Drive, the quiet residential street in front of the resort, and a palm-lined walkway takes you right down to the sand. No shuttle, no long walk. Complimentary beach chairs and umbrella are available in the condo — bring them down with you each morning. Prefer the full setup? Beach chair and umbrella rental is also available for purchase directly on the beach from local vendors.

### Resort Amenities

Beyond the front door, the Majestic Sun / Seascape Resort community layers a Gulf-front outdoor pool, an indoor heated pool with cathedral ceilings, hot tubs, a full fitness center, tennis and pickleball courts, Seascape Golf Club, and walking paths onto a quieter, greener stretch of coast. The result is a relaxed resort atmosphere with exceptional amenities while maintaining the quieter feel that makes Miramar Beach so popular. Golfers seeking an 18-hole round: Emerald Bay Golf Club is about 3 miles away.

### The Neighborhood

- **Grocery:** Publix at Grand Boulevard (725 Grand Blvd, Miramar Beach) — ~5 min drive · Winn-Dixie on Poinciana Blvd is closer (~1 mile) for a quick run
- **Coffee:** 2 Birds Coffee & Café at Seascape Town Center — walkable
- **Casual dinner:** Whale's Tale Beach Bar & Grill (walkable via beach), Boshamps Seafood, Diego's Burrito Factory
- **Upscale dinner:** Marlin Grill, Baytowne Wharf restaurants
- **Family activity:** Village of Baytowne Wharf (seasonal fireworks), Big Kahuna's Water & Adventure Park, Silver Sands Premium Outlets (~1 mile east)
- **Nature:** Henderson Beach State Park, Topsail Hill Preserve State Park

**Nearest Airports**

- Destin–Fort Walton Beach Airport (VPS) is approximately 40 minutes (24 miles) by car.
- Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport (ECP) is approximately 1 hour (38 miles) away. *⚠ verify exact drive time from the property before publishing.*

### About Your Stay

Check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM. Complimentary covered parking is included for registered guests. On departure, please run the dishwasher, take trash to the chute, and leave used towels in the tub — that's it.

### About Your Host

Westlight is personally hosted by Simone. I've been welcoming guests to Florida's Emerald Coast for more than six years — I personally handle every booking, every guest message, and every arrival detail—never a third-party management company. I typically reply within 2 hours. You'll get detailed arrival instructions about a week before your stay, and I'm a message away during your trip if anything comes up. I don't hover — but I'm here.
