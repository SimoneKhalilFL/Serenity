## Headline — locked ship string *(Phase 3 initiative #1, 2026-07-09)*

**`Westlight · Gulf-Front 2BR · Sleeps 6 · Miramar Beach`** *(53 / 80 chars)*

**Copy this exact string into the VRBO listing editor's `Headline` field.** Middots are `·` (U+00B7), not periods. No trailing punctuation.

**Ship rationale:**

- **Same brand prefix as the Airbnb title.** The first 26 characters (`Westlight · Gulf-Front 2BR`) match the Airbnb title exactly — deliberate. A guest who cross-shops the same brand across Airbnb and VRBO (which many do, especially for high-consideration bookings) reads the same brand+differentiator on both platforms and recognizes it as the same listing. Reduces the "are these the same place?" confusion that kills conversions.
- **VRBO extension adds sleeps + city.** VRBO surfaces the city less prominently than Airbnb on search tiles, so we include `Miramar Beach` in the headline itself. `Sleeps 6` speaks to VRBO's core audience (families and groups booking a whole home, filtering by guest count).
- **Middot separators throughout.** Consistent visual rhythm; same glyph as the Airbnb title.
- **Under the 80-char hard limit.** The full string runs 53 chars — well inside the hard cap and inside the ~60-char practical mobile-tile window. The `Westlight · Gulf-Front 2BR` opening runs 26 chars — under the soft 40-char threshold that keeps the brand + core differentiator visible on truncated tile views.
- **No fluff, no adjectives.** `Luxury`, `Beautiful`, `Stunning`, `Family Retreat` — all deliberately absent. VRBO's search-signal weight is on specifics (bedroom count, sleeps, city, feature) not adjectives (per the [`Poor examples`](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-I-list-my-property) VRBO itself calls out in its listing guidance).
- **Portfolio consistency.** Twenty First's VRBO headline uses the same formula: `Twenty First · Gulf-Front 3BR · Sleeps 8 · Panama City Beach`. Same shape, different city + bedroom + sleeps values.

**Owner sign-off:** 2026-07-09 evening.

**Never use:** floor number (8th, 8th-floor, floor 8), unit number (811, MS811, condo 811), retired brand names (`Majestic Sun 811` as guest-facing brand, `Fun in the Sun`, `Serenity`), resort name as a lead (`Majestic Sun Resort · Westlight …`), all-caps, exclamation marks, or promotional language (`Book Now`, `Best rate`, `Special offer`).

## Long Description

### Welcome

At Westlight, every evening ends in gold. This 2-bedroom, 2-bath Gulf-front retreat sits on the west-facing edge of Miramar Beach, where the emerald water meets sugar-white sand and the last light of the day pours across the balcony. A slower, quieter stretch of Florida's Emerald Coast — designed to be lived in barefoot, coffee in hand, sunset ahead.

### The Home

Westlight is a 2-bedroom, 2-bath condominium that comfortably sleeps up to 6:

- **Primary bedroom:** King bed with Gulf-facing windows
- **Guest bedroom:** Queen bed, tucked away for privacy
- **Living room sleeper sofa:** Queen sleeper; fits 1–2 additional guests
- **Sleeps up to 6 guests total**

Inside, the kitchen is fully stocked for six — coffee maker, cookware, and dinnerware for real cooking, not just reheating. Dining table seats six. Smart TVs in every bedroom and the living room with streaming (Netflix, Prime, Disney+). Wi-Fi reaches every room. In-unit washer and dryer.

### The View & Beach

The private Gulf-front balcony is why guests come back. Morning coffee with dolphins in the surf line. Afternoons to the sound of waves. Golden hour that gives the property its name. Direct beach access is a palm-lined boardwalk downstairs — no crossing a road. Complimentary beach chairs and umbrella are available in the condo — bring them down with you each morning. Prefer the full setup? Beach chair and umbrella rental is also available for purchase directly on the beach from local vendors.

### Resort Amenities

Beyond the front door, the Majestic Sun / Seascape Resort community layers a Gulf-front outdoor pool, an indoor heated pool with cathedral ceilings, hot tubs, a full fitness center, tennis and pickleball courts, a championship golf course (Seascape Golf Club), and walking paths onto a quieter, greener stretch of coast. It's a full-service resort setting — but with a boutique-community rhythm rather than a boardwalk one.

### The Neighborhood

- **Grocery:** Publix at Silver Sands Premium Outlets — ~5 min drive
- **Coffee:** 2 Birds Coffee & Café at Seascape Town Center — walkable
- **Casual dinner:** Whale's Tale Beach Bar & Grill (walkable via beach), Boshamps Seafood, Diego's Burrito Factory
- **Upscale dinner:** Marlin Grill, Baytowne Wharf restaurants
- **Family activity:** Village of Baytowne Wharf (seasonal fireworks), Big Kahuna's Water & Adventure Park, Silver Sands Premium Outlets
- **Nature:** Henderson Beach State Park, Topsail Hill Preserve State Park
- **Airport:** VPS (Destin–Fort Walton Beach Regional) — ~15 minutes

### About Your Stay

Check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM. Covered on-site parking is included. On departure, please run the dishwasher, take trash to the chute, and leave used towels in the tub — that's it.

### About Your Host

Westlight is personally hosted by Simone. I've been welcoming guests to Florida's Emerald Coast for more than six years — every booking, every message, every arrival detail is personally mine, not a management company's. I typically reply within 2 hours. You'll get detailed arrival instructions about a week before your stay, and I'm a message away during your trip if anything comes up. I don't hover — but I'm here.

- Never claim awards, rankings, or "Premier Host" badges we don't hold. (Simone does hold Premier Host — that is a real host-level badge and is fine to reference in the description body.)
