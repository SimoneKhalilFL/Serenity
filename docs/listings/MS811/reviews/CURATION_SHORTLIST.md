# MS811 (Westlight) — Curation Shortlist (2026-07-10)

**Purpose:** Owner-reviewed shortlist of 28 verified OTA reviews to publish on the Westlight property page (`stayatflorida.com` → `#lodging` → Westlight card → detail page). Selected from the 86-review archive in [`2026-07-10-vrbo.md`](./2026-07-10-vrbo.md) and [`2026-07-10-airbnb.md`](./2026-07-10-airbnb.md).

**Policy alignment (portfolio parity with TW2111):**

- **Rating policy** — Only real max-rating reviews are published (VRBO 10/10, Airbnb 5★). Every `rating: 5` on the site corresponds to the maximum score on the source OTA. No rating manipulation.
- **Naming policy** — First-name-only rendering. `author` field on the site is the first name as it appears on the source OTA (e.g., "Jacqueline R." → `Jacqueline`, "maria b." → `Maria` [title-cased]). `sourceName` preserves the source-published form for audit.
- **Aggregate policy** — Site emits `AggregateRating` for the published set (28 reviews, all 5★ → 5.0). The broader 86-review archive averages lower (VRBO 9.8/10 = 4.9-eq, Airbnb 4.86/5) and is NOT claimed as an aggregate on the site. Honest against the published set, no marketing inflation.
- **Content edits** — Only two types of edits are applied to verbatim source text: (1) forbidden-language removal per portfolio §21 (unit/floor numbers), and (2) minor typo/punctuation cleanup. No sentiment changes, no rewording. All edits documented in the notes column below.

**Total selected:** 28 (17 VRBO + 11 Airbnb) — mirrors TW2111's 25-review shortlist proportionally, adjusted for MS811's larger archive.

**Guest favorites:** 1 (`Jeannie M.` — combined view + fully-stocked-kitchen theme; strongest signal on both dominant loved-for chips).
