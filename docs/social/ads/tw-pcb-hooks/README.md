## Why this shelf exists

StayAtFlorida has **two** beachfront homes. Westlight already has a locked lifestyle set (morning balcony). This shelf is the matching Panama City Beach campaign for Twenty First — same hook language, PCB branding, dual goals:

1. Make people want the stay (emotion first).
2. Train them to remember **StayAtFlorida.com** (not only Airbnb / VRBO).

Do **not** run a feed that looks like a property-management catalog. Mix lifestyle, destination, personality, proof, education, and conversion per the 30-day calendar.

---

## Creative rules

| Surface | What to post |
|---|---|
| **Feed / Reels (preferred)** | Clean photos or short clips. Hook text as **on-screen Meta text** or caption — not burned into the still. |
| **Stories / ads / email / print** | The flyer-style PNGs in this folder are fine. |
| **Never on lifestyle stills for Feed** | Giant “BOOK NOW,” amenity icon stacks, or StayAtFlorida.com as the main visual. Luxury travel account first; advertisement second. |

**Reel formula (repeat):**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0–1 sec | Gulf, balcony, or dramatic room reveal — **not** the logo |
| 1–3 sec | Hook text |
| 3–7 sec | 2–4 quick property / experience clips |
| Final | `Panama City Beach, FL · StayAtFlorida.com` |
