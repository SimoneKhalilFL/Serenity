> **Rule (2026-09-02):** A **unique Reel every day at 5:00 PM** CT for the **same property** as that day’s feed + Story. Graph publishes it. Never reuse an MP4 or its source stills.
>
> Each Reel publishes to **Facebook + Instagram** (Business Suite → Create reel).
>
> **Media for now:** Ken Burns stills (no phone clips yet). Files in [`docs/social/schedule-ready/reels/`](../schedule-ready/reels/). Regenerate: `python scripts/generate-reels.py`

## Reel formula (every file)

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0–1 sec | Gulf / balcony / room reveal — **never the logo** |
| 1–3 sec | Hook as **on-screen text in Meta** (do not rely on burned flyer text) |
| 3–7 sec | 2–4 quick stills with slow zoom |
| Final | Caption CTA: `Panama City Beach, FL · StayAtFlorida.com` or `Miramar Beach, FL · StayAtFlorida.com` |

**Audio default:** no music on Ken Burns still Reels (owner direction 2026-09-01). Optional later: calm Meta library track only — skip party/trending audio. Real Gulf ocean sound when phone clips exist. Share to Story on.

## How to schedule a Reel in Business Suite

1. Asset picker: **Stayatflorida + stayatflorida** (never Custom Wreath)
2. **Create reel** (not Create post)
3. Upload from `docs/social/schedule-ready/reels/`
4. Add **on-screen text** for the hook in the first 1–3 seconds
5. Paste caption + hashtags
6. **Set date and time** → that day **5:00 PM** (FB + IG)
7. Share to Facebook Story → on
8. Groups same as still posts
9. Schedule — dismiss Boost with Maybe later
