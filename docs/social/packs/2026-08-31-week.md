## How to publish in Business Suite

1. Open [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/)
2. Left asset picker → **StayAtFlorida, stayatflorida** (not Custom Wreath)
3. **Create post**
4. Add photo (download from the URL above)
5. Paste caption (Customize off unless FB/IG must differ)
6. Destinations: **Facebook Page + Instagram**
7. **Share to Facebook Story** — always on
8. **Post to Facebook groups** — select the saved StayAtFlorida groups (list in [`docs/brand/SOCIAL.md`](../../brand/SOCIAL.md))
9. Schedule for the day’s slot (or Publish now)
